## 💰 Economics

### Pricing — matches live [`/pricing`](https://hyper-vibe-coding-course.vercel.app/pricing)

| Tier | Price | Audience | Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | £0/mo | ~80% | Free lesson previews, XP tracking, leaderboard |
| Pro | **£9/mo** | ~15% | All courses + BROski$ + certs + community |
| Hyper | **£29/mo** | ~5% | Everything in Pro + agent sandbox + 1:1 monthly review |

**Parallel one-time revenue:** 7 individual courses available à la carte (£19.99–£49.99) for buyers who don't want a sub. Stripe is wired live for both checkout flows.

### True Monthly Costs (corrected)

```
Vercel Pro:           £20.00
Supabase Pro:         £25.00
Stripe fees:          ~2.5% of MRR (UK rate + £0.20/txn)
Anthropic API:        ~£0.50/Hyper user/mo (conservative, light usage)
Domain + misc:        ~£3.00
─────────────────────────────
Fixed minimum:        £48/mo
Variable:             ~3% of MRR + £0.50 × Hyper users
```

### Break-Even (corrected)

- **Conservative (3% conv):** Month 2–3 — needs ~6 Pro subs to clear fixed + variable
- **Target (5% conv):** Month 1 still possible at ~150 free signups

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Solo founder bandwidth | High | 4-repo ecosystem already shipped — agent infra reduces ops load. Discord moderators recruited from Hyper tier. |
| AI cost variability (Anthropic) | Medium | Per-user spend tracked. Hyper tier priced with 3× headroom. Hard caps + caching live. |
| Free→paid conversion below 3% | Medium | Plan break-even off conservative line. First 5 students free for 6 months → testimonials → conversion lift. |
| Stripe / Vercel concentration | Low | Both have 99.9%+ uptime track records. Webhook idempotency + Supabase rollback queue already live. |
| Churn above 20% | Medium | BROski$ + pet evolution + streaks = compounding switching cost. Monthly cohort review starting Month 1. |
| Discord community doesn't form | Medium | Founder-led for first 90 days. Hyper sponsors incentivised to seed activity. |
