## 📖 SECTION 1 — What Makes an App Have Personality (0:40 – 3:30)
### 🎬 SCREEN: Slide — "4 ingredients of personality"

---

Four things separate a personality-filled app from a boring one.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: "1. COLOUR PALETTE" — with bad vs good example

One — Colour Palette.

[PAUSE]

Instead of "use blue and white" — boring.
Use "cyberpunk neon pink and deep purple."
Or "cottagecore forest green and warm cream."

[PAUSE]

Colours tell a STORY.

[PAUSE]

Your colour palette is the first thing someone feels.
Before they read a single word.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: "2. TYPOGRAPHY" — with bad vs good example

Two — Typography.

[PAUSE]

Instead of Inter font — everyone uses it.
Use a futuristic display font for headings.
Clean readable body text underneath.
Or a handwritten-style font for warmth.

[PAUSE]

Fonts are the VOICE of your app.

[PAUSE]

They speak before your content does.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: "3. MICRO-INTERACTIONS" — show an animation demo

Three — Micro-interactions.

[PAUSE]

Instead of "click button, it works."
Add: button scales up on hover.
A tiny ding sound.
An emoji celebration when you complete something.

[PAUSE]

These are details.
But they're what people FEEL.
They're what makes someone smile.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: "4. VISUAL DETAILS" — show before/after

Four — Visual Details.

[PAUSE]

Instead of basic flat shapes — add gradients.
Add shadows. Borders. Custom icons.

[PAUSE]

Details are what make people REMEMBER your app.

[PAUSE]

Apps with personality have all four.
Boring apps have none.

[PAUSE]

## 📖 SECTION 2 — What is a Design System (3:30 – 6:00)
### 🎬 SCREEN: Show Apple's design system as an example

---

A design system sounds intimidating.

[PAUSE]

It's not.

[PAUSE]

It's just — rules you follow consistently.

[PAUSE]

Instead of each page looking different —
everything follows the same rules.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: Apple example with annotations

Look at Apple.

[PAUSE]

Every product. Every screen. Every button.
Consistent colours. Consistent fonts. Consistent animations.

[PAUSE]

That's why something looks — Apple-like.
You know it when you see it.

[PAUSE]

You need YOUR version of that.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: Simple design system example — four rules

Your design system is four rules.

[PAUSE]

Always use THESE colours.
Always use THIS font pairing.
Always use THIS spacing.
Always use THIS animation speed.

[PAUSE]

Follow those four things — and everything you build
looks like it came from the same mind.

[PAUSE]

YOUR mind.

[PAUSE]

## 📖 SECTION 3 — Think in Feelings (6:00 – 9:30)
### 🎬 SCREEN: Four feeling quadrants

---

Stop thinking about "professional" or "modern."

[PAUSE]

Those words mean nothing.
Every app claims to be professional and modern.

[PAUSE]

Instead — think in FEELINGS.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: Quadrant 1 — "Energetic + Fun"

Energetic and fun.
Bright colours. Playful fonts. Bouncy animations.
Think: Duolingo. Bright greens, celebrations, confetti.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: Quadrant 2 — "Calm + Focused"

Calm and focused.
Muted colours. Clean fonts. Slow animations.
Think: Notion. Minimal. Nothing fighting for your attention.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: Quadrant 3 — "Dark + Edgy"

Dark and edgy.
Neon on dark backgrounds. Bold fonts. Glitch effects.
Think: VSCode. Dark. Sharp. Powerful.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: Quadrant 4 — "Warm + Cozy"

Warm and cozy.
Earth tones. Rounded shapes. Soft shadows.
Think: Bear notes app. Warm. Inviting. Safe.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: All four quadrants visible

Pick ONE feeling.

[PAUSE]

Commit to it for your entire app.

[PAUSE]

Consistency IS personality.

[PAUSE]
