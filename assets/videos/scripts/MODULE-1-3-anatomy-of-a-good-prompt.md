## 📖 SECTION 3 — The Three Ingredients (2:30 – 4:30)
### 🎬 SCREEN: Slide — "The Prompt Formula"

---

Every great prompt has three ingredients.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: "1. ROLE" appears

One — ROLE.

[PAUSE]

"You are a designer who..."
"Build this like a startup would..."

[PAUSE]

This gives AI your vibe and style direction.
It changes HOW it thinks about the problem.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: "2. CONTEXT" appears

Two — CONTEXT.

[PAUSE]

"For a coffee shop manager in New York..."
"Built for someone who hates spreadsheets..."

[PAUSE]

AI understands WHAT you're building and WHO it's for.
This shapes every design decision.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: "3. TASTE" appears

Three — TASTE.

[PAUSE]

"Make it feel calm, cozy, warm. Use earth tones."
"Bold and energetic. Like a nightclub meets a tech startup."

[PAUSE]

AI knows HOW it should FEEL — not just function.

[PAUSE]

When you nail all three?

[PAUSE]

Magic.

[PAUSE]

## 📖 SECTION 4 — Red Flags to Avoid (4:30 – 5:30)
### 🎬 SCREEN: Two-column — Bad vs Good

---

Quick cheat sheet. Stick this on your wall.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: Bad column populates

Don't say "make it professional." That's vague.
Don't ask for ALL colours at once. Pick two or three.
Don't over-specify technical code. Let AI decide HOW.
Don't forget personality hints. AI defaults to boring.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: Good column populates

Say "make it feel playful, not corporate."
Say "neon pink and deep purple on black."
Say "I trust you to add cool animations."
Say "make it look like a high-end design studio."

[PAUSE]
