## 📖 SECTION 2 — The Five-Part Formula (2:00 – 5:30)
### 🎬 SCREEN: Formula slide — five parts numbered

---

Every great interactive prompt has five parts.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: "1. ROLE + CONTEXT" with example

One — Role and Context.

[PAUSE]

"You're building a fitness app for people with ADHD.
I want a simple workout timer that keeps people motivated."

[PAUSE]

This sets the whole tone.
AI now thinks like a designer who understands ADHD.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: "2. CORE INTERACTION" with example

Two — Core Interaction.

[PAUSE]

"When I click Start Workout — the timer begins.
Every ten seconds — show an encouraging message.
When I finish — celebrate me with confetti."

[PAUSE]

Describe what happens WHEN users act.
Not how to code it. What to DO.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: "3. STATE" with example

Three — State.

[PAUSE]

"Show my total minutes exercised this week.
Show my current streak.
Show completion percentage toward my weekly goal."

[PAUSE]

What should the app REMEMBER?

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: "4. EDGE CASES" with example

Four — Edge Cases.

[PAUSE]

"If I close mid-workout — resume where I left off.
If I hit pause — pause it, don't reset it."

[PAUSE]

This is where most beginners skip.
And then get confused when AI misses scenarios.

[PAUSE]

Tell AI what happens when things go wrong.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: "5. TASTE" with example

Five — Taste.

[PAUSE]

"Make it energetic, not stressful.
Use bright colours. Fun.
Make me feel proud, not judged."

[PAUSE]

The emotional target.
How should someone FEEL using this app?

[PAUSE]

## 📖 SECTION 3 — A Real Example (5:30 – 7:15)
### 🎬 SCREEN: Full example prompt — food diary app — annotated

---

Let me show you all five parts in one real prompt.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: Prompt appears section by section with colour-coded labels

Role + Context:
"You're building a food diary app for people
who want to track nutrition without obsessing."

[PAUSE]

Core Interaction:
"When I log a meal — show me the macros in a chart.
Celebrate if I hit my goals.
Don't shame me if I don't."

[PAUSE]

State:
"Remember all meals this week.
Show a weekly summary: 75% of goals hit."

[PAUSE]

Edge Cases:
"If I log the same meal twice — ask me to confirm.
If I haven't logged today — remind me gently."

[PAUSE]

Taste:
"Make it supportive, not judgmental.
Use warm colours — orange, cream.
Include positive reinforcement and humour."

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: All five parts highlighted together

That's the full formula.
Role. Interaction. State. Edge cases. Taste.

[PAUSE]

Every interactive app you build from now on.
