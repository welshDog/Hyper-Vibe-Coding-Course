## 📖 SECTION 1 — The Wrong Way (0:30 – 1:30)
### 🎬 SCREEN: Highlight the bad phrase "make it look nice"

---

Stop saying this.

[PAUSE]

"Make it look nice."
"Make it modern."
"Make it professional."

[PAUSE]

Those words mean NOTHING to AI.

[PAUSE]

AI doesn't know what "nice" means to you.
It defaults to whatever is most average.
That's why you get grey backgrounds and Arial font.

[PAUSE]

## 📖 SECTION 3 — The Aesthetic Prompt Checklist (3:30 – 5:30)
### 🎬 SCREEN: Checklist slides — each point appears with a tick

---

Six things to include in every aesthetic prompt.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: Each appears with a checkmark

One. Colour palette.
"Use my colour palette: dominant, secondary, accent."
Exact hex codes. Not descriptions.

[PAUSE]

Two. Fonts.
"Display font: Space Grotesk. Body font: Inter."
Names, not descriptions.

[PAUSE]

Three. Spacing rules.
"Use 8px grid. 16px between sections. 12px padding on buttons."
AI respects numbers.

[PAUSE]

Four. Animation style.
"Fast animations, 150ms, snappy and bouncy."
Or "slow, smooth, 400ms."

[PAUSE]

Five. Design references.
"Make it feel like Notion, but more colourful."
"Like Duolingo, but darker."

[PAUSE]

Six. What NOT to do.
"Avoid Arial. Avoid grey backgrounds. Avoid flat buttons."
Constraints help as much as instructions.

[PAUSE]
