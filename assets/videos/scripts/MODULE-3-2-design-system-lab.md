### 🎬 SCREEN: Show the 60/30/10 rule diagram

Here's the rule.

[PAUSE]

One DOMINANT colour. Sixty percent of your app.
One or two SECONDARY colours. Thirty percent.
One ACCENT colour. Ten percent — buttons, highlights, calls to action.

[PAUSE]
