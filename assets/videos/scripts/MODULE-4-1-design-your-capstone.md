## 📖 SECTION 1 — What Makes a Good Capstone (0:45 – 2:30)
### 🎬 SCREEN: Three criteria appear

---

Three criteria for a good capstone.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: "1. You'd actually use it"

One — you'd actually use it.

[PAUSE]

Not built to impress me.
Built because a version of you tomorrow needs it.

[PAUSE]

The best apps are built by frustrated people.
Someone who keeps forgetting to do something.
Someone who can't find the right tool.
Someone who thinks "why doesn't this exist?"

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: "2. Fits in one page"

Two — fits in one page.

[PAUSE]

No login system. No database. No API calls.
Local storage is fine.

[PAUSE]

This is Week 4, not Year 4.
Simple and shipped beats complex and broken.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: "3. Has a clear job to do"

Three — has a clear job to do.

[PAUSE]

Describe it in one sentence.
"An app that helps me track X when I Y."

[PAUSE]

If you can't say it in one sentence — it's too complex.
Simplify until you can.

[PAUSE]

Mine:

One — Daily brain dump notepad that resets at midnight.
Two — Just me.
Three — I type → it saves. I close → it remembers. Midnight → it clears.

## 🔧 WRITING THE PROMPT (8:00 – 9:30)
### 🎬 SCREEN: Build the prompt from the plan

---

Now take your answers and write the prompt.

[PAUSE]

### 🎬 SCREEN: Type the prompt

```
You are a designer building personal productivity tools.

I want a daily brain dump notepad:
- One big text area — type anything
- Auto-saves as I type (local storage)
- Shows today's date at the top
- At midnight, automatically clears and starts fresh
- Simple "Clear now" button if I want to reset early
- Shows a word count at the bottom

DESIGN SYSTEM:
Colours: Black #0a0a0a, purple #9333ea, cyan #06b6d4
Fonts: Space Grotesk display, Inter body
Feel: Minimal, focused, dark. Like a hacker's notebook.
No distractions. Nothing unnecessary.
Animations: Simple, fast, 150ms.

Avoid: White backgrounds, bright colours, clutter.

Give me complete HTML + CSS + JS code.
```

[PAUSE]
