# 🧠 Skill: neurodivergent-pedagogy

Teaching rules for ADHD + Dyslexia + Autistic learners.
Every page, every module, every prompt must follow these rules.

---

## Core Philosophy

> "Stop apologising for your brain. Start building."

This course is for people who've been told they're "too much" or "not enough".
The brain differences here are SUPERPOWERS, not limitations.

- ADHD → hyperfocus engine, pattern-spotting, systems thinking
- Dyslexia → big-picture thinking, creative problem solving
- Autistic → deep expertise, consistency, systems mastery

---

## The 7-Part Module Structure

Every single module/page MUST follow this exact order:

| # | Section | Purpose |
|---|---------|--------|
| 1 | **STOP** | Plain English context BEFORE any tech. No jargon. |
| 2 | **WHY** | Real-world use case. Use Netflix, Uber, Stripe refs. |
| 3 | **HOW** | Step-by-step with ⏱️ time estimates on each step. |
| 4 | **WIN** | Clear, celebratable moment + explicit recognition. |
| 5 | **NEXT** | Warm, friendly bridge to the next section. |
| 6 | **HELP** | Troubleshooting that NORMALISES problems. |
| 7 | **REWARD** | BROski$ XP claim. Always at the end. |

---

## Writing Rules (Non-Negotiable)

✅ Short sentences. Max 15 words per sentence.
✅ One idea per paragraph. Max 3 sentences.
✅ Bullet points over walls of text. Always.
✅ Bold the key word in every bullet.
✅ Use emojis as visual anchors — not decoration.
✅ Time estimates on EVERY step: ⏱️ 5 min
✅ Celebrate every tiny win explicitly.
✅ "You" language — speak directly to the learner.

❌ No jargon without a plain-English definition first.
❌ No steps longer than 3 sentences.
❌ No walls of text (more than 4 lines without a break).
❌ No passive voice.
❌ No "simply" or "just" — it invalidates struggle.
❌ No assuming the learner knows the prerequisite.

## Momentum Design

- **Quick wins first.** Each level must have a win within the first 10 minutes.
- **Chunked content.** No section longer than a 5-minute read.
- **Visual progress.** Always show where the learner is in the journey.
- **Locked levels.** Reveal the next level only after the current win is claimed. Builds dopamine loop.
- **Celebrate loudly.** "Nice one BROski♾️!" is always the right response to a win.

---

## Troubleshooting Tone

When something goes wrong, ALWAYS:
1. Normalise the problem first: "This trips everyone up."
2. Give the fix in 1-2 sentences max.
3. End with reassurance: "You're still on track."

NEVER:
- Blame the learner
- Use language that implies failure
- Give a 10-step fix when 2 steps will do

## Content Labels for Pages

Use these visual labels to help learners navigate:

- **DO THIS FIRST** — the single most important step
- **OPTIONAL** — extra depth for those who want it
- **ONLY IF YOU'RE HOOKED** — deep dives
- **⏱️ X min** — time estimate on every step
- **🔒 LOCKED** — next level, claim reward to unlock
- **✅ DONE** — completed step or section
