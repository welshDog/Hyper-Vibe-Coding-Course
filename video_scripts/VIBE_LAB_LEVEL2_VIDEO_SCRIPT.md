**🔧 HOW**
Your first full-stack build. Four steps.
**Step 1:** Open Build Mode.

**Step 2:** Describe your idea.
Plain English. Be specific about the look and the goal.
**Step 3:** Refine in chat.
Change colours, add features, adjust the layout — just by typing.
**Step 4:** Use Annotation Mode.
Click the thing you want changed. Describe it. Gemini updates it live.
Try this prompt:
A Hyperfocus Timer. Dark background, purple.
A 25-minute focus timer. A 5-minute break.
Start and Stop. A session history list with timestamps.
