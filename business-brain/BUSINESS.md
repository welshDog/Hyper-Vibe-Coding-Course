## 🎯 TARGET AUDIENCE

**Primary:** ADHD, dyslexic, autistic adults (18–45) who want to learn to code
- Frustrated with traditional bootcamps and Udemy-style courses
- Need chunked, visual, rewarding learning
- Respond to community, gamification, and quick wins
- Often in the UK but global audience

**Secondary:** Neurotypical learners who prefer the ND-friendly style
**Tertiary:** Employers/orgs looking to upskill ND staff
