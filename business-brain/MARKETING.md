## 🎯 Who We're Talking To

**The Primary Avatar: "Alex"**
- 24–38 years old, UK-based (but global)
- ADHD and/or dyslexic and/or autistic
- Has tried to learn coding before and quit
- Feels like traditional courses "weren't built for their brain"
- Loves Discord, Reddit, TikTok, YouTube
- Has heard of Docker and AI but finds it overwhelming
- Wants to build something real — not just complete exercises
- Responds to: humour, validation, quick wins, community belonging
- Rejects: gatekeeping, jargon walls, "you should already know this" energy

---

## 🗣️ Tone of Voice Rules

### ALWAYS:
- Casual and mate-style — "hey bro", "let's crack on", "nice one!"
- Short sentences first, detail after
- Celebrate small wins loudly
- Use emojis to chunk content (🔥 ✅ 🧠 💜 🚀)
- Validate the struggle before offering the solution
- "Stop apologising for your brain. Start building."

### NEVER:
- Walls of text
- Condescending "as you may know..." framing
- Jargon without plain English explanation
- Pressure tactics or fake urgency
- "This course will make you rich" energy
- Ableist framing — ADHD/dyslexia/autism = SUPERPOWER, not limitation

## 🏷️ Launch & Discount Rules

- **Founding Member discount:** Max 25% off, first 100 students only, time-limited
- **Never discount publicly** without a clear reason (devalues brand)
- **Referral program:** Give referring student 200 BROski$ per successful referral
- **Bundle deals:** Upgrade pricing (pay difference to move tiers) — handle via Discord
- **Always frame discount as reward**, not desperation: "You're early. Here's your Founding Member rate."
